Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Morsel is built to keep your recipes on your phone. We collect as little as possible, we don't sell your data, and we never show you ads. This policy explains exactly what happens to the information you give us - and which features share anything at all.

The short version: Your recipes, folders, meal plans, trackers and settings live on your device. When you save a recipe from a link, or use an AI feature, only what's needed for that task is sent to our servers and the providers that power them. Nothing is public unless you choose to share it to the community. An account is optional: it lets your Morsel Plus subscription follow you and, if you turn on sync, keeps your recipes on every phone you sign in on. Your food and water logs, goals and body details never leave your device.

1. Who we are

"Morsel", "we", "us" and "our" refer to the makers of the Morsel mobile app. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at morselrecipeapp@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Stored on your device

The following stay on your phone and are not sent to us, except where a feature below says otherwise (for recipes and folders, that's only if you turn on sync):

  • Your recipes and folders - titles, ingredients, steps, servings, macros, notes, source links and photos, kept in a local database and image cache.
  • Your profile & preferences - the display name you choose, plus your theme (System / Light / Dark), unit (Metric / Imperial) and language settings.
  • Your Plus tools - food and water you log, your daily goals, and the age, sex, height and weight you enter into the goal calculator. These never leave your device.
  • Your meal plans, grocery lists and reminders - including your shopping trip time and reminder schedules.
  • Backups. Morsel keeps an automatic copy of your recipes in the app's private storage, and you can export a backup file to anywhere you choose (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email). That file is yours - we never receive it.

Sent to our service when you save or improve a recipe

  • The link you paste. When you add a recipe, the URL (an Instagram or TikTok post, or a recipe blog/website) is sent to our extraction service so it can fetch and read that page.
  • The page content. The caption, comments, transcript, text and images found at that link are processed to produce structured recipe data and macro estimates. For some Instagram and TikTok videos with no usable text, the video itself may be analysed by an AI model.
  • Recipe text for AI tools (Morsel Plus). If you use the AI Kitchen Assistant, ingredient substitutes or Adjust to Taste, the recipe's ingredients and steps, and what you ask for, are sent to an AI model to produce the answer.
  • Ingredient and photo lookups. Ingredient names are sent to nutrition databases when you use the optional nutrition cross-check, and a search phrase based on the recipe title is sent to Pexels when a recipe needs a photo. Ingredient names may also be sent to an AI model to sort a grocery list by aisle.
  • Smart Planner. To fill a week of meals, the app asks our service for recipes that fit your diet preferences and calorie range. Your body details and food log are not sent.

Shared recipes and the extraction index

To make Discover useful, recipes extracted from public links (a public Instagram or TikTok post, or a recipe web page) are kept in a shared, searchable index so other Morsel users can find and save them. The index stores the recipe and a public link to its source - it is not linked to the person who saved it. Links that look private (for example, ones carrying a login or tracking token) are never published.

Community: ideas board and recipe sharing

The community features are optional and public. If you take part, you are choosing to publish:

  • Ideas board: posts, comments, votes and reports. Posts are shown without a name.
  • Recipe sharing in Discover: the recipe you share, your note, any photo you attach, and your display name (the first name from your profile, or a generated handle like "Cook 1A2B" if you haven't set one). Comments and suggestions on shared recipes show the same name.

You must accept the community rules before you can post. Text and photos are checked by automated moderation (an AI model) before publishing, and other users can report content. We can also see an anonymous, one-way code derived from your device so we can remove abuse and block repeat offenders - it is never shown to other people. You can delete your own posts, shared recipes and comments in the app.

Anonymous install ID

Morsel creates an anonymous identifier for your install so we can enforce the monthly AI-extraction limit, redeem Plus codes, and keep "your" community posts yours without an account. On Android it is a one-way hash of the device's app-scoped Android ID; on iOS it is a random value kept in the Keychain. We never receive the raw hardware identifier, it is not an advertising ID, and we don't use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Optional account & Morsel Plus

Morsel works fully without an account, and the core app stays free. If you choose to sign in:

  • Account. You can sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple. We use Supabase to manage sign-in. We receive your email address (or Apple's private relay address), the name the provider shares, and a unique user ID.
  • Subscriptions. If you subscribe to Morsel Plus, payments are handled by RevenueCat and the app store (Google Play or the App Store). We receive your subscription status - plan, store, product and expiry date - not your card details. If you redeem a Plus code, the code is linked to your install ID.
  • Sync across devices (Morsel Plus, opt-in). Sync is off until you turn it on in Backup & Restore. When it's on, your recipes (titles, ingredients, steps, servings, macros, notes, source links and category), your folders and which recipes are in them, and your recipe photos are stored with your account in Supabase, so they appear on every phone you sign in on. Photos are kept in private storage that only your account can read. Your food and water logs, goals, body details, meal plans, grocery lists, reminders and settings are not synced and stay on your device. Deleting a recipe on one phone deletes it from the cloud copy and your other phones. Turning sync off stops further syncing; deleting your account deletes the synced copy.

Device permissions

Morsel asks only when a feature needs it, and each is optional:

  • Photos / camera - to add a picture to a recipe. Photos stay on your device unless you share that recipe to Discover or turn on sync (then a smaller copy is stored privately with your account).
  • Notifications - for water, meal, grocery and shopping-trip reminders. These are scheduled locally on your phone; Morsel does not use remote push, so no notification data is sent to us.
  • Reminders (iOS) - to add your grocery list as reminders when you tap export. Morsel writes the items you ask for and does not read your existing reminders.
  • Calendar - when you add a shopping trip, Morsel opens your phone's own calendar screen so you can confirm the event.

What we do not collect

  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs are built into the app.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not handle or store your payment card details.
  • We do not read your contacts, location, or existing calendar or reminders.

3. Third-party services we use

Our backend relies on a few specialised providers. Only the data needed for each task is shared with them:

  • Apify - fetches the content of Instagram and TikTok links.
  • Google (Gemini) - turns scraped page content, and in some cases video, into structured recipe data; powers the AI Kitchen Assistant, substitutes and Adjust to Taste; classifies grocery items; and moderates community posts and photos.
  • DeepSeek - analyses recipe text (captions, comments, transcripts) when it is available, to extract recipes at lower cost.
  • Pexels - finds a relevant stock photo when a recipe has no image of its own.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts - public nutrition databases used, only if you opt in, to cross-check macros.
  • Supabase - sign-in, subscription records, community content, the public image storage used for shared recipes, and - if you turn on sync - the private copy of your recipes, folders and recipe photos.
  • RevenueCat - processes and manages Morsel Plus subscriptions together with your app store.
  • Apple and Google - provide Sign in with Apple / Google sign-in, the app stores, and billing.
  • Upstash and Render - infrastructure that runs our service: short-lived usage counters and caches, and server hosting, which may log technical details such as your IP address.

These providers process data on our behalf for these purposes and each handles data under its own privacy policy. Our servers and providers may be located in countries other than your own.

4. How we use your information

  • To extract recipe details and estimate macros from the links you save.
  • To run the AI, planner and grocery features you ask for.
  • To backfill a photo when a recipe doesn't include one.
  • To enforce fair-use limits, prevent abuse, and keep the community safe.
  • To verify your Morsel Plus subscription and restore it on a new device.
  • To let you organise, search, scale, convert and cook your saved recipes.

We do not use your recipes or links to build advertising profiles, and we do not use your personal recipe library to train models.

5. Data retention

Your recipes, folders, profile, trackers and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Deleting a recipe removes it, and its cached image, from your device - and, if sync is on, from the cloud copy and your other phones.

  • Extraction and AI caches hold the results of processed links and requests for a limited time, so repeated requests are quicker. Usage counters reset monthly.
  • Shared index entries from public links stay in Discover's index and are not tied to you.
  • Community content you publish stays visible until you delete it or we remove it under the community rules.
  • Synced recipes are kept with your account until you delete them or delete your account. A deleted recipe leaves only a small marker (its ID, when it was deleted, and - for a duplicate merged into another copy - which one) so your other phones know to remove it too; its content and photo are removed.
  • Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your sign-in, your subscription record, and any synced recipes, folders and photos from our database and storage. Billing records held by RevenueCat, Apple and Google are retained under their own policies and legal obligations.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Delete anything, anytime. Remove individual recipes or folders in the app, or uninstall to remove everything stored locally.
  • Delete your account in the app. If you created an account, open Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account on our servers, including any synced recipes and photos; the recipes on your phone stay. It does not cancel a store subscription - cancel that first in Google Play or the App Store.
  • Delete what you shared. Remove your own ideas, shared recipes and comments in the community screens. To have something else removed, email us.
  • Opt-in features. The nutrition cross-check, community posting, sharing, accounts, sync, reminders and Morsel Plus all run only when you choose to use them.
  • Backups are yours. Export and import your recipe backup file whenever you like.
  • Offline use. Once saved, recipes can be browsed and cooked with no network connection at all.
  • Access & questions. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to our use of your data. Email morselrecipeapp@gmail.com and we'll help with any privacy request.

7. Children's privacy

Morsel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The community features are for people aged 13 and over.

8. Security

Recipe extraction and AI features run on a backend so that provider API keys stay off your device. Data sent to and from that service is transmitted over encrypted connections, sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage, and our database tables are locked down so that apps cannot read other users' data. No method of storage or transmission is ever 100% secure, but we design Morsel to minimise the data at risk in the first place.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of Morsel after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy? We'd genuinely like to hear from you at morselrecipeapp@gmail.com or through the contact form. See also our Terms of Use.
