How it works

Three taps from link to dinner

No re-typing, no screenshots buried in your camera roll. Just paste and go.

1

Paste a link

Drop in an Instagram or TikTok post, or any recipe blog URL. That's the whole job.

2

Morsel reads it

It extracts the title, servings, ingredients and steps - and estimates the macros when they aren't listed.

3

Cook it anywhere

Everything is saved on your phone, organised and searchable. Cook mode walks you through it, step by step.

Feature showcase

Everything a recipe hoarder needs

Built for the way you actually save and cook - warm, fast and yours.

Save

Get a recipe out of any post or page and onto your phone - macros included.

Save from any link

Instagram, TikTok and recipe blogs. Morsel prefers structured recipe data, falls back to page text, and can even watch a video when the caption has no recipe.

Share straight in

Hit Share on any Instagram, TikTok or web page and pick Morsel - the recipe is saved without leaving the app you were in.

Automatic macros

Calories, carbs, protein and fat on every recipe - estimated when the source doesn't state them.

USDA cross-check

Optionally recompute macros from parsed ingredient weights against USDA FoodData Central.

Cook

Everything you need at the stove, even with no signal.

Cook mode

A full-screen, one-step-at-a-time view with tap-to-advance zones, a progress bar and an ingredient peek.

Scale & convert

A serving stepper live-scales quantities and macros, with metric ⇄ imperial conversion built in.

Adjust to taste Plus

Dial any ingredient up or down - sugar, salt, gluten, dairy, spice and more - and the rest of the recipe is rebalanced to match.

Kitchen assistant Plus

Smart ingredient swaps and healthier versions of any recipe you've saved.

Works offline

Recipes, folders, meal plans and images live on-device. Only saving or discovering new recipes needs the network.

Light & warm dark

A warm, food-forward theme in light and dark, with metric or imperial units - your preference, remembered.

Speaks your language

The app interface is available in English and Español, while each recipe stays faithful to its original source.

Organise

A cookbook that stays tidy, findable and safe.

Categories & folders

Meals, Drinks, Snacks and Desserts out of the box, plus custom folders for your own collections.

Search everything

Find recipes by title, ingredient, step, note, category, folder or source link - instantly.

Backup & restore

Your cookbook is mirrored automatically, and you can export one backup file to Files or Drive and import it on a new phone.

Plan & share

Plan the week, shop once, and see what other cooks are making.

Smart Planner Plus

A week of meals planned around your daily calorie goal, drawing on your cookbook first.

Grocery lists Plus

One tidy, aisle-sorted list from your meal plan, with shopping-trip and weekly reminders. Export to Reminders on iPhone or Calendar on Android.

Calories & water Plus

Log what you eat - by hand or straight from a saved recipe - and what you drink, against daily goals, with a progress card on your home screen.

Discover & community

Search recipes other cooks have saved or shared, remix them, and suggest improvements. Vote on ideas for Morsel's next features.

Nutrition

Know the macros before you cook

Every saved recipe gets a clean macro summary. When a source leaves them out, Morsel estimates - and you can cross-check against the USDA database for the real numbers.

Morsel Plus

A little more Morsel, for the everyday cook

The Morsel you love stays free. Morsel Plus is an optional upgrade for people who want tracking, planning and smarter help in the kitchen.

  • Calorie & water tracking, with a goal calculator and gentle reminders
  • Smart Planner and aisle-sorted grocery lists built from your week
  • An AI kitchen assistant, substitutes and "adjust to taste" tweaks
  • Advanced nutrition breakdowns and 200 AI imports a month (free: 30)

Monthly or yearly, with a free trial where offered. Auto-renews until you cancel in Google Play or the App Store. Sign in (optional) so Plus follows you to a new phone. Cross-device recipe sync is still on the way.
