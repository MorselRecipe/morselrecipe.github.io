2. What Morsel is

Morsel is a mobile app that saves recipes from links, keeps them on your phone, and helps you organise, scale, cook, plan and track them. It includes optional features: AI recipe extraction, nutrition estimates, a Smart Planner and grocery lists, reminders, a community ideas board, recipe sharing and Discover, and an optional paid plan called Morsel Plus. Features may change, and some listed as "coming soon" are not promised and may not ship.

5. Nutrition, health and food-safety disclaimer

Macros, calories, diet tags, goals and meal plans in Morsel are estimates. They are produced by AI models, public nutrition databases and standard formulas, and they can be incomplete or wrong. They are not medical, dietary or allergy advice.

  • The calorie goal calculator is a starting point based on a standard equation. Talk to a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet, especially if you have a health condition.
  • AI suggestions - substitutes, "adjust to taste", healthier variants and the Kitchen Assistant - may change a recipe in ways that affect taste, texture, safety or cooking time.
  • Always check ingredient labels and confirm allergens yourself. Cook food to safe temperatures and follow good food-handling practice.

You use Morsel's recipes and nutrition information at your own risk.
